# My Career Readiness — GoMyCode Bootcamp

## Introduction

My name is Marvin, and I am currently learning full-stack software development through the GoMyCode bootcamp. This experience has helped me understand how websites and web applications are created, and it has encouraged me to keep improving my skills as a developer.

## Skills I Have Gained

During the bootcamp, I have been learning and practising:

- **HTML:** Building the structure of web pages.
- **CSS:** Styling pages and working towards responsive designs for different screen sizes.
- **JavaScript:** Using variables, data types, arrays, objects, functions, conditions, and events to make pages interactive.
- **React:** Creating components and working with JSX and state.
- **Node.js and Express:** Understanding the basics of servers and backend development.
- **Git and GitHub:** Tracking changes, storing projects, and sharing my work.
- **Problem-solving:** Breaking programming tasks into smaller steps and learning from errors.

## How Ready Am I for the Next Step?

I feel motivated and increasingly prepared to take the next step in my software development journey. I know that I am still learning, and I do not consider myself an expert yet. However, I am becoming more comfortable writing code, understanding how different technologies work together, and building projects that demonstrate what I have learned.

One project I am working on is **SocialDesk**, a simple social media content management prototype. It gives me an opportunity to practise building interfaces and organizing features such as clients and posts. My goal is to continue developing it into a more complete full-stack application.

## Challenges and Lessons

One of my challenges has been creating responsive layouts with CSS. I have also found some JavaScript and React concepts difficult at first. When I get stuck, I revisit my lessons, practise again, ask questions, and try to understand why the code works rather than only copying it.

The bootcamp has taught me that software development requires patience, consistency, research, and continuous practice.

## My Career Goals

My goal is to become a skilled **full-stack software developer** who can build useful, professional web applications. I want to strengthen my frontend and backend skills, build a portfolio of real projects, collaborate with other developers, and eventually work with clients or companies.

## Conclusion

I am ready to keep growing and take on more challenging projects. The GoMyCode bootcamp has given me a foundation, and I understand that becoming a good developer will require continued learning and hands-on experience.
